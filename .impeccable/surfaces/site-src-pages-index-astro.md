# Surface brief: Apex Cybersecurity marketing site (home + all routes)

Scope: full multi-page marketing site in site/ (Astro). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: UK public-sector and business buyers (procurement, IT managers, heads of security) shortlisting a cyber security supplier for Apex Cyber Security, Sevenoaks, Kent.
Action: start a conversation (contact / call), or forward a service page into a bid file.
Proof on hand: none yet beyond services and contact details. No clients, stats, testimonials, people or imagery: never invent; mark supplier facts as slots. Nothing from the US firm used as a model may appear.
Memorable moment: the CSF wheel in the first viewport that answers "what do you cover" for any function in one click.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Apex covers every NIST CSF 2.0 function (Govern at the hub, then Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) as one supplier, so the site is a framework profile. It refuses the category default: dark data-center photo, neon padlock, card grid of services.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground (#f5f6f3), navy ink (#0b1f33). The six CSF function colors (gold GV, blue ID, purple PR, orange DE, red RS, green RC) are the whole palette and fill full-bleed bands at full saturation. Public Sans (the U.S. Web Design System face) for text, Archivo at expanded widths for display, Red Hat Mono only for outcome IDs and the clock. No cards or boxes: a baseline grid, hairline table rules, weight and color carry hierarchy. Figures and tables are numbered and captioned.

STORY: The visitor sees in one viewport that Apex covers the whole framework, picks the function they're buying for, sees the exact services, checks certifications and leadership, then calls or writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top strip with a live 24-hour watch line (UK time, "Security operations on watch") and the incident phone number. Nav below. Left 5/12: headline "Govern to Recover. One certified partner.", one sentence of support, primary button "Start a conversation" and secondary "Call (312) 566-7491", UK office line. Right 7/12: Fig. 1, the interactive CSF wheel (Govern hub plus five wedges), about 560px, with the selected function's services listed beside or below it.

FORM: NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 profile documentation, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key a476efd3. Raises: color fills whole regions (from metro tiles); always-visible "now" via the watch line (from the step row); no enclosing boxes (from the cracktro); numbered figures (from the curved-crease shell). Signature interaction: the wheel, whose segments draw in on load, and selecting a function re-indexes the service list. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, segment extrusion, one orchestrated entrance only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
