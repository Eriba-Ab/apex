## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), in `site/`. Chosen by the user from "a framework (Astro/Next)"; Astro because this is a content-led marketing site with no app logic. Hosted on Netlify.

## Users

Primary buyers are UK public-sector organisations and businesses looking for a cyber security supplier: procurement officers, IT managers and heads of security who need a supplier they can justify in a tender or supplier file. They are evaluating, not browsing: they want the scope of services, credentials and a way to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Apex Cyber Security is a cyber security firm based at 52 High Street, Sevenoaks, Kent TN13 1JG. It offers consulting, assessments and compliance readiness; 24/7 managed detection and response; endpoint detection and response; DevOps and secure application development; network infrastructure; integrated security systems (access control and CCTV); mobile surveillance trailers; and cyber security training. The website's job is to make Apex an easy, credible choice and to start a conversation.

## Positioning

One supplier for cyber, network and physical security, with every service organised around the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 so buyers can see coverage at a glance.

## Operating Context

Buyers read the site while building supplier shortlists and tender responses, and forward service pages to colleagues. Incident callers need the phone number immediately.

## Independence from the US model

A Chicago firm with a similar name (Apex Cybersecurity Solutions, apexcybersecurity.org) was used only as a structural model. Nothing identifying it may appear on this site or in its source: no names, photographs or biographies of its people, logo, imagery, trademarks or registered terms, slogans, blog posts, partner relationships, training programme branding, identifiers (NAICS, UEI, CAGE, contract vehicles) or copied text.

## Product Principles

1. Tender-ready: every service page answers "what exactly do you do" in a form a buyer can paste into a shortlist.
2. Credentials stated plainly, and only when confirmed; unconfirmed facts stay as visible slots.
3. One supplier across cyber, network and physical security: the breadth is the differentiator, so show it as one system.
4. Never invent proof.
5. The incident line is always one tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public-sector buyers commonly require WCAG 2.1 AA. Treat AA as the floor.
